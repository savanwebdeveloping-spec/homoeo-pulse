Privacy Policy for Homoeo Pulse

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Welcome to Homoeo Pulse ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of BHMS (Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery) medical students, educators, and clinicians who use our mobile and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Homoeo Pulse app, we may collect the following information:

  • Profile Information: Your name, BHMS academic year, enrolled homeopathic medical college, and optional student email.
  • Academic Activity: Reading progress, bookmarked remedy chapters, organon aphorisms, and offline downloaded study materials.
  • Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, and anonymous performance logs to ensure smooth app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized academic curriculum materials matching your BHMS year (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Year, or Internship).
  • To sync your reading progress and bookmarked notes across your sessions.
  • To send AYUSH university exam updates, date sheets, and syllabus announcements.
  • To improve our medical study content and app stability.

3. Offline Storage & Security

Homoeo Pulse includes an offline study vault allowing students to study without an active internet connection. Downloaded documents and reading preferences are stored securely in the app's local sandbox storage.

4. Third-Party Services

We may integrate trusted third-party providers including:

  • Google Play Services: For application distribution, crash analysis, and updates.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: For hosting verified AYUSH textbooks and university notes.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can edit or reset your profile data anytime within the Profile section of the app. If you wish to delete your stored profile completely, you can use the "Reset Profile" feature or contact our support team.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for medical students enrolled in undergraduate and postgraduate BHMS programs and is intended for individuals aged 17 and above.
